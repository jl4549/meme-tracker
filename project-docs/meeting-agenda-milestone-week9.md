# Milestone meeting with Max — agenda (Week 9)

## 1. Research direction
**One line:** We study how a meme's meaning drifts as it spreads: which versions change its conclusion, where AI steps in, and whose accounts steer it.

- **RQ1 Conclusion drift:** How does what each version concludes relate to the original's conclusion (same / extended / shifted / contradicted)? What information is gone when they diverge?
- **RQ2 People × AI:** Where do people and AI meet (AI-made versions, AI remakes of older memes, AI answers in threads), and how do these moments redirect the meme?
- **RQ3 Account power:** Do large, small or official accounts shape which conclusion others follow?
- Framework: design-space map (rows = evolution over time; columns = platforms and languages).

**Outcomes**
- **A. Meme lookup tool:** input a meme → the story behind it + its trace map.
- **B. Power map:** who shapes a topic's narrative (e.g. 2024 politics).
- Plan: A is the main product; B is a layer on A (nodes coloured by account power).

**Ask Max:** Is the direction clear and focused enough? A + B combined, or A only for now?
