“Leaked photo of heaven is going viral on social media”

An image that many people called AI-made was shared as a real photo of heaven. On Bluesky it was quoted and replied to hundreds of times. The versions split two ways: TYPE Apicture-based, working on the original picture, and TYPE Btopic-based, keeping the sentence and bringing a new picture. Those are two of several ways to sort them. Here are 27 versions. Each one shows what it kept, what it added, and what it lost.
